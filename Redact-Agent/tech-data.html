Extra load the extension adds on top of a plain Chrome tab.

While a redaction runs, the agent uses about 258 MiB extra RAM and adds roughly 12 percentage points to peak CPU. Both fit comfortably in the 2 GiB / 50% headroom a normal workstation has.

Conclusion

What the numbers mean in practice.

Result: Passed

Across 30 runs, resource use stays steady:

  • CPU: Peaks stay below 33% and the average under load is 15.86%.
  • Memory: Idle footprint is around 670 MiB PSS. Giving the process about 1,200 MiB total (about 500 MiB of headroom) is enough to cover the peak.
  • Cleanup: Residual PSS averages +0.39 MiB with no upward trend. No leak.
