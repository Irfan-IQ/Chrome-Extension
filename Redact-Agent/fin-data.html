Summary

Final Verdict

What the numbers add up to.

Bottom Line

Launch costs about $145. Running costs are $2,800–$4,200/yr at 1,000 users and $5,700–$8,700/yr at 10,000 users on a self-hosted Qwen backend. 10× the users costs only about 2× more.

Per-User Cost

$0.23–$0.35/user/mo at 1K users, falling to $0.05–$0.07 at 10K users. A $1/user/mo price keeps gross margin above 90%.

Software Choices

AWQ-INT4 quantisation shrinks Qwen2.5-7B to ~6 GB and roughly doubles throughput. vLLM continuous batching holds GPU use near 90% at peak.

Scale Behaviour

At 10K users the GPU runs close to full; at 1K users it sits around 15%. Non-GPU items grow slower than user count.
