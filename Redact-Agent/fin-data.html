Final Verdict & Key Takeaways

High-level financial conclusions for stakeholders and founders.

Executive Bottom Line

"Launch outlay is ~$145[cite: 5]. Steady-state software spend is ~$2,800–$4,200/yr at 1,000 users and ~$5,700–$8,700/yr at 10,000 users on a self-hosted Qwen backend—representing a highly efficient ~2x cost increase for a 10x user increase[cite: 5]."

Per-User Economics

Costs range from $0.23–$0.35/user/mo at 1K users, dropping sharply to $0.05–$0.07 at 10K users. Monetising at even $1/user/mo yields >90% gross margin[cite: 5].

Software Multipliers

AWQ-INT4 quantisation shrinks Qwen2.5-7B to ~6 GB and doubles throughput. vLLM continuous batching keeps GPU utilization near ~90% at peak[cite: 5].

Economies of Scale

GPU hardware is near-fully utilized at 10K users (vs ~15% at 1K), and non-GPU line items scale sub-linearly, maximizing financial efficiency[cite: 5].
